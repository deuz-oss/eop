# Target Customer

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the target customers of the Enterprise Operations Platform (EOP).

The objective is to ensure that every product decision, feature, workflow, and user experience is optimized for organizations managing distributed field operations.

---

# Primary Target Customer

Organizations with field employees that require daily operational planning, execution, monitoring, and performance management.

Typical industries include:

- FMCG
- Retail
- Distribution
- Pharmaceutical

These industries become the initial market because they have:

- Large numbers of field employees
- Daily store visits
- Territory management
- Performance targets
- Operational supervision requirements

---

# Customer Problems

The platform is designed to solve:

- Limited visibility of field operations
- Manual reporting
- Slow operational decisions
- Poor execution consistency
- Difficult KPI monitoring
- Inaccurate field data
- Lack of accountability

---

# Non Target Customer

EOP is not intended for:

- Manufacturing production management
- Accounting firms
- Banking operations
- E-commerce operations
- Companies without field employees

---

# Product Design Principle

Every feature must answer one question:

"Does this improve field execution?"

If the answer is no, the feature should not become a priority.
