# Product Principles

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

These principles guide every product decision.

Whenever uncertainty exists, these principles take precedence.

---

# Principle 1

Execution Driven

Every feature must improve field execution.

---

# Principle 2

Operational First

Operational reliability is more important than visual complexity.

---

# Principle 3

Offline First

Field employees must remain productive even with unstable internet connections.

---

# Principle 4

Simple by Default

The product should be easy to learn with minimal training.

---

# Principle 5

Enterprise Ready

Designed for organizations with hundreds or thousands of users.

---

# Principle 6

Auditability

Important actions must be traceable.

---

# Principle 7

Configurable

Business rules should be configurable whenever practical.

Avoid unnecessary hardcoding.

---

# Principle 8

API First

Every business capability should be accessible through APIs.

---

# Principle 9

Performance Matters

Users should never wait longer than necessary.

Fast software improves adoption.

---

# Principle 10

Security by Design

Security is part of the architecture, not an afterthought.
